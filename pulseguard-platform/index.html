THE ENGINEERED SOLUTION

Our Solution: IIOT Condition Monitoring System

A practical, open, multi-device condition monitoring platform engineered to address every industrial challenge.

01

Multi-Device Individual Monitoring

Every motor has its own individual panel and sensors (DS18B20 digital temperature, MPU6050 vibration RMS, ACS712 line current). Track multiple machines concurrently across your workshop.

02

Temperature Maintenance & Manual Override

Users can specify target maintenance temperatures and alarm thresholds. Includes a dedicated manual value setting mode to inject custom test values and verify cooling interlocks.

03

Machine Schedule & Electricity (₹ INR)

Configurable shift timings (Start, End, Days/Week) and electricity tariffs in ₹ INR / kWh. Automatically calculates daily/monthly power costs and wear-and-tear index to schedule cooling rest.

04

Comprehensive PDF Audit Summary

1-click PDF download compiling fleet summaries, working timings, historical readings, and an explicit register of every abnormal activity and anomaly across all machines.

05

Hardware Sensor Fault Isolation

Intelligent logic distinguishes between actual mechanical degradation and an instrument wiring disconnect. Prevents false plant shutdowns when a sensor wire loosens.

06

Hardware Node Cost Under ₹800 INR

Replaces proprietary ₹5,00,000 systems with standard, reliable ESP32 microcontrollers and commodity industrial sensors for unmatched cost-effectiveness.
